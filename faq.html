Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting in touch.

What is Spectrum Bridge?

Spectrum Bridge is an international network connecting LGBTQ+ individuals across Africa with information, pathways and support — spanning employment, education, relocation, protection and international opportunities.

Who can get in touch with Spectrum Bridge?

LGBTQ+ individuals across African countries. Our network is not limited to one nationality, country or community.

Which African countries are included?

We work with people across the continent, including Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Uganda, South Africa, Tanzania, Rwanda, Zambia, Zimbabwe and many other African countries.

Is there a cost to reach out?

No. Getting in touch with Spectrum Bridge through this website is free. We don't collect payments or personal information through this site.

What happens after I get in touch?

Our team will respond and, from there, you can share more about your circumstances, interests and skills. We'll discuss relevant pathways directly with you and, where appropriate, make trusted referrals or introductions.

Does contacting Spectrum Bridge guarantee an opportunity?

No. Getting in touch starts a conversation — it doesn't guarantee a specific outcome. Any relevant possibilities are discussed individually, based on your circumstances and eligibility.

Is Spectrum Bridge an official immigration or asylum service?

No. We share information and connect people to relevant pathways and networks, but we are not a government body or a substitute for formal legal, immigration or asylum advice. For legal matters, please consult a qualified professional.

How is my information protected?

We understand the sensitivity of what we do. Any information shared with our team directly is treated with care and confidentiality — and you're never asked to provide more personal information on this public website than you're comfortable sharing. If you prefer, use secure channels when providing sensitive details.
