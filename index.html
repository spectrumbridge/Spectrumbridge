How It Works Get in Touch

Your journey starts here

Spectrum Bridge is an international network connecting LGBTQ+ individuals across Africa with opportunities, pathways and support that can open new possibilities for the future — whether that means employment, education, protection, relocation or access to LGBTQ+ resources.

We work with people based on their individual circumstances, interests and skills — because no two journeys look the same.

Spectrum Bridge is built to create meaningful connections between LGBTQ+ individuals across Africa and the networks that can help them move forward. It starts with a conversation.
