How Spectrum Bridge works

A simple, direct process — no forms to fill in on this site, just a real conversation with our team.

01

Learn about Spectrum Bridge

Read through our site to understand who we are, who we serve, and the areas we connect people to — from employment to protection and beyond.

02

Get in touch

Reach out to our team directly using the contact details on our Contact page. You don't need to share sensitive details in your first message.

03

Share your circumstances

Once we've responded, you can share more about your situation, interests, skills and experience so our team can understand how best to help.

04

Review and discussion

Our team reviews what you've shared and discusses relevant pathways and next steps with you directly.

05

Ongoing contact

If a relevant possibility is identified, we'll stay in touch with you about eligibility and the next steps involved.
