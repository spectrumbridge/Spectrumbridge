Building bridges. Creating possibilities.

Spectrum Bridge is an international LGBTQ+ network connecting people across Africa to a wider network of opportunities, pathways, programs and support — including local assistance and international referrals.

Who We Are

An international LGBTQ+ network

We believe that where someone is born or where they currently live should not limit their ability to pursue a better future. Spectrum Bridge was created to build bridges between communities and networks that can offer practical support and genuine opportunities for safety, education, employment and relocation.

People who reach out can share their circumstances, interests and skills, and become part of a network where they can be considered when relevant possibilities arise.

Our Mission

To connect. To support. To create possibilities.

Our mission is to create a trusted bridge between LGBTQ+ individuals across Africa and the networks, opportunities and resources that can help them move forward. We prioritise safety, confidentiality and practical outcomes in everything we do.

Our Vision

A world without borders for LGBTQ+ possibility

We envision a world where LGBTQ+ individuals can access meaningful opportunities and support regardless of where they come from. Spectrum Bridge seeks to build partnerships and pathways that reduce barriers and expand access to education, employment, protection and relocation options.

What We Do

How the network works

Spectrum Bridge provides a network through which LGBTQ+ individuals can get in touch and share their circumstances directly with our team. From there, information is reviewed and we connect people to trusted partners, referrals and resources that are relevant to their situation.

Our network covers areas including jobs and employment, asylum and protection, relocation, education and scholarships, skills and training, international opportunities, and financial or other support where available.

Who We Serve

LGBTQ+ people across Africa

Spectrum Bridge is open to LGBTQ+ individuals across African countries. Our network is not limited to one nationality, country or community — our goal is to create pathways that can help people find safer, more hopeful futures.

Our International Network

Connecting Africa to the wider world

Spectrum Bridge is built with an international outlook. Our focus begins with Africa, but our vision extends beyond borders — through our growing network, we aim to link people to opportunities and partners worldwide.

Spectrum Bridge is the bridge between where you are and where possibilities may lead you.
